Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy governs the use of the ShortsFactory Automation tool ("the Application"), developed by Esad Sahin / Feyne Web Yazilim. The Application is an automated desktop workflow designed to render and upload vertical video content to YouTube.

1. Information We Access via Google / YouTube APIs

When you authorize ShortsFactory using Google OAuth 2.0, the Application requests permission to interact with your YouTube account under the following Google API scopes:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload: Used solely to upload owner-created video files and set video metadata (title, description, tags, category).
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly: Used solely to verify the authentic YouTube Channel ID and ensure videos are published to the correct designated channel.

2. How Information is Used and Stored

The Application is a strictly self-hosted desktop tool. All OAuth authentication tokens, refresh tokens, and video files are stored locally on the user's personal workstation.

  • We do NOT operate external cloud databases or third-party servers that collect or aggregate your YouTube data.
  • We do NOT sell, license, rent, or share any personal information, Google user data, or YouTube channel metrics with any third parties or advertisers.
  • All video generation, text synthesis, and voice generation run completely locally.

3. Google API Limited Use Disclosure

ShortsFactory's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy , including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Data Retention and Revocation

You can revoke ShortsFactory's access to your Google Account at any time through Google's Security Settings page at: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the local token files stored on your workstation will permanently erase all local authentication credentials.
